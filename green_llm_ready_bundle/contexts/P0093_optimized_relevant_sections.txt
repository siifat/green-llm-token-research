TITLE: Techniques for Reducing the Cost of Generative-AI Inference: A Structured Review
AUTHORS: Garcia, P.; Kim, J.
YEAR: 2026

2. RELATED WORK
Theme 1: Model compression. Quantization, pruning, distillation, and sparse activation reduce computation or memory use. Studies often report speed and memory, while energy reporting is less consistent.

Theme 2: Serving-system optimization. Dynamic batching, speculative decoding, kernel fusion, and cache-aware scheduling improve accelerator utilization and tokens per second.

Theme 3: Workload reuse. Prefix caching, semantic caching, retrieval, and response reuse avoid repeated computation when inputs overlap.

Theme 4: Carbon-aware operation. Geographic routing, time shifting, and renewable-energy matching aim to reduce emissions even when energy use changes little.

Across themes, studies use incompatible quality metrics and hardware, limiting direct comparison.
