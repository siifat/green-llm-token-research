4. RESULTS
Standalone correctness was 82%; RAG correctness was 93%. Unsupported claims fell from 14% to 4%. On 7% of questions, retrieval failed to return the necessary passage, and those failures accounted for most incorrect RAG answers.
6. CONCLUSION
Instructor-approved retrieval substantially improved course-answer factuality and reduced unsupported claims, with retrieval quality becoming the main remaining bottleneck.
